WHO WE ARE

Your stay. Your story. Our coast.

Takaye Huts is a registered Kenyan real estate enterprise offering handpicked holiday homes and property solutions along Kenya’s coast. From beachside villas and cosy cottages to modern apartments, we make it easy to find a place that feels like yours, whether you’re here for a weekend escape, a family holiday, or a long stay.

What we offer

  • Holiday apartments, villas and cottages in Malindi, Watamu, Kilifi, Mombasa and Diani.
  • Conference and event venues for meetings, retreats, workshops and celebrations.
  • Commercial and residential properties for businesses, investors and long-term residents.
  • Curated coastal experiences, from dhow sailing and snorkelling to guided trips to Gedi Ruins and Vasco da Gama Pillar.

Why guests choose us

  • Local knowledge from a team that lives and works on the coast.
  • Clear pricing and simple booking.
  • Properties cared for and ready on arrival.
  • Personal support from enquiry to check-out.

We want every guest and client to feel welcomed, informed and supported as they find their place on the coast.
